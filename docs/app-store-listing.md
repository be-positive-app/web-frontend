## Full description (both stores, up to 4000 characters)

### EN
Be Positive helps you plan your day and get things done, on your own or with your team at work.

FOR YOUR DAY
- Daily tasks with reminders, so nothing slips
- A calendar that shows the week at a glance
- Focus tools and streaks that keep you going
- Progress you can see: what you finished today, this week, this month

FOR YOUR WORK
If your company uses Be Positive Teams, your work tasks come to this app too. No computer, no extra logins.
- See what to do, by when, and for whom
- Mark it done, add a photo, hand it in for sign-off
- Stuck? Press "I'm blocked", pick a reason, and your manager knows the same minute
- Get thanks from colleagues and badges like Employee of the Month
- Join your company with an invitation e-mail or the company code

Your manager runs the company from the web panel at web.bepositive.cc. You do your part from your phone.

Available in English, Azerbaijani, Russian and Turkish.
